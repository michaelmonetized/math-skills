Call the Skill tool twice, for "epsilon-delta-grilling" and "domain-of-discourse".

Whenever a question in the grilling turns on what is already known ("hasn't someone proved this for p = 2?", "what does the standard reference assume?"), dispatch a background sub-agent with the "researxiv" skill rather than answering from memory, and keep grilling the rest of the frontier while it reads. Bring its verified findings back into the next round.
